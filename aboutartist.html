    Artist Statement

    My mixed media artworks are influenced by literature, folklore, nature, and the human condition. I work with symbolism, mantras, figures, faces, and fantasy creatures. Throughout my works, I explore the contrast of innocence and darkness that plays on the edge of every human story.

    I want to connect the viewer to their own ability to find redemption and magic through that visual balance of light and darkness; perhaps imbuing the artworks with my own intentions of power for the viewer to incorporate within themselves.

    My work often returns to themes of transformation, identity, and the interplay between reality and imagination. I am fascinated by the stories that emerge from the subconscious and the ways in which personal and collective histories shape our perception of the world.

    Bio

    Sara McKee is a Houston native, visual artist and digital designer working with diverse media. Her practice draws on a background in art education, and she brings the same curiosity and care for people to both her studio work and her design work.

    Outside the studio, she travels, practices yoga, and shares her home with her husband and two cats.
