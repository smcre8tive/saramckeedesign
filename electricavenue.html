01
Background

The Challenge

Electric Avenue is a small local electric bike shop that did not have the bandwidth to maintain their digital pressence. The owners also managed two other stores in Austin and Dallas so their bandwidth was limited for interacting with digital customers.The challenges included updating their older wordpress site inventory regulalry, maintain their online presence using Meta For Business and responding to Google reviews.

How can we streamline Electric Avenue's digital presence with Wordpress, Meta Business and Google Reviews to better serve electric avenue customers?

02
Discovery

Research & Insights

Primary research included daily retail clientel interactions and observations for four months and monthly social insight data.

Key Insights:

  • Most store e-bike customers are men or familiesaged 26-41 and prefer to browse the physical inventory in store and online but purchase bikes over the phone with the store owners.
  • Customers are looking for a seamless experience across all touchpoints.
  • Customers value personalized white glove service and supporting local businesses.
  • Customer interactions were primarily in-person at the retail store, so the digital presence needed to support and extend that experience rather than be a standalone sales channel.
  • Social media organization, branding and engagement were lacking, due to lack of time for consistent posting keeping potential digital customers in the dark.
